# Color Tokens — ComplyWise Design System

## Palette Overview
The ComplyWise palette is measured directly from the visual reference. It features airy light neutrals, crisp card whites, subtle sage green, vibrant lime highlights, and deep charcoal/slate accents.

## Hex Specifications & Roles

### Base & Backgrounds
- **Outer Canvas**: `#edf0f6` — Neutral cool gray desktop backdrop
- **Application Window Frame**: `#f6f7fb` — Soft off-white window surface
- **Card Background**: `#ffffff` — Crisp white for cards
- **Subtle Surface**: `#f1f5f9` — Controls, icon containers, secondary pills

### Semantic & Accents
- **Applicable Requirements Card (Sage Green)**: `#d5e2d8`
  - Purpose: Special thematic card representing statutory coverage and BIS authority.
- **Lime Accent**: `#ecfa98` / `#eefbaa`
  - Usage: Highest activity day highlight (Friday bar), growth badges (`+32%`), pinned chart milestones (`3 actions`).
- **Teal / Mint Accent**: `#82ded8` / `#a2e4df`
  - Usage: Central 82% compliance bubble mark.
- **Teal / Mint Translucent**: `rgba(228, 249, 245, 0.7)`
  - Usage: Secondary 10% bubble mark.
- **Warning / Star Yellow**: `#fef08a`
  - Usage: Standard highlight badge (`★ IS 3055`).
- **Alert Red**: `#ef4444`
  - Usage: Negative trend or action required (`↑ 2 from last week`, notification badge).
- **Progress Green**: `#10b981` / `#a7f3d0`
  - Usage: Verified documents bar, positive indicators (`↗ 3 this week`).

### Typography Hierarchy
- **Heading & Numbers (Primary)**: `#0f172a` — High contrast, deep slate/black.
- **Body & Subtitles (Secondary)**: `#475569` — Medium slate for secondary labels and subtitles.
- **Subtle & Captions (Tertiary)**: `#94a3b8` — Card footers, auxiliary metadata, x-axis day labels.

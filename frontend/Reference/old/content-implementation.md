# ComplyWise — Content Implementation Plan

## 0. Goal

Replace the current technically-heavy landing-page copy with a calm, minimal, trust-first voice for a normal business user.

The content should explain the product without asking the visitor to understand regulatory technology terminology.

The visitor should progressively understand:

```text
I have a business.
↓
Compliance depends on my business details.
↓
ComplyWise understands those details.
↓
It asks only for what is missing.
↓
It shows what applies.
↓
It shows why.
↓
It turns the result into next steps.
```

---

# 1. Audience

Primary:

- business owners
- founders
- operations heads
- manufacturers
- MSMEs
- exporters
- compliance teams

Secondary:

- CAs
- CSs
- consultants
- industrial ecosystem teams

The landing page should be understandable without prior knowledge of RAG, embeddings, ASTs, or deterministic engines.

---

# 2. Voice

## Use

- calm
- direct
- clear
- confident
- human
- respectful
- specific
- reassuring

## Avoid

- hype
- fear
- overpromising
- legalistic prose
- architecture jargon
- AI buzzwords
- unnecessary technical terminology

## Voice test

Ask:

> Would a business owner understand this sentence on the first read?

If not, simplify it.

---

# 3. Core Content Rule

Use:

> **One memorable sentence + one useful explanation + one visible product action.**

Do not use:

> headline + dense paragraph + second dense paragraph + technical disclaimer.

The animation should carry part of the explanation.

---

# 4. Content Hierarchy

## Layer 1 — Story

Large, memorable statements.

Examples:

- Behind every business is a rulebook.
- Start with what you do.
- Only answer what changes the answer.
- See why it applies.
- Turn requirements into next steps.

## Layer 2 — Explanation

One short supporting sentence.

## Layer 3 — Product UI

Technical terms are allowed here:

- source
- rule
- threshold
- exemption
- effective date
- evidence
- verification

## Layer 4 — CTA

Use verbs:

- Explore
- See
- Understand
- Start
- Review

Avoid:

- Transform
- Revolutionize
- Unlock the future
- Supercharge

## Chapter 01 — Understand


## Chapter 02 — Adaptive Questions

### Headline

**Only answer what changes the answer.**

### Body

**When an important detail is missing, ComplyWise asks for it. Nothing more.**

### Question card

Use a realistic business question.

Example:

**What is your annual turnover?**

The card can show:

```text
Why we are asking
This detail can change the requirements we show you.
```

Do not say:

- decision-critical unknown detected
- statutory path routing
- variable MSME turnover bracket

Those may remain as tiny technical metadata if needed.

---

## FAQ

Use plain business questions.

Recommended questions:

1. **How does ComplyWise know what applies to my business?**
2. **What happens when you need more information from me?**
3. **Can I see where a requirement came from?**
4. **What does ComplyWise help me do after a requirement is found?**
5. **Which businesses and jurisdictions are currently covered?**
6. **Does ComplyWise replace professional legal or compliance advice?**

Answers should be 2–4 sentences, plain English, factual, and calm.

# 6. Current-to-New Content Transformation Rules

| Current Style | New Style |
|---|---|
| Industrial compliance fails when businesses are forced into rigid, generic forms. | **Start with what you do.** |
| Canonical operational facts | **Business details** |
| Decision-critical unknown detected | **We need one more detail.** |
| Dynamic fact resolution | **Ask only what matters.** |
| Statutory relevance mapping | **Relevant requirements** |
| Regulatory discovery & ingestion pipeline | **Find the rules that matter** |
| Deterministic rule engine | **See what applies** |
| The rule executes against verified facts. | **We check the requirement against your business details.** |
| The evidence chain & provenance | **See why it applies** |
| Synthesized compliance action item | **Next step** |
| Product payoff | **Put the result to work** |
| Audit governance | **How we keep information trustworthy** |
| Architectural truth | **How ComplyWise works** |
| Separation of cognition and determinism | **AI understands. Rules guide the decision.** |

The table is a direction for rewriting, not a literal requirement to replace every phrase word-for-word.

# 7. Content That Can Remain Technical

Technical terms can stay in small UI areas where they add credibility.

Examples:

```text
RULE-014
JURISDICTION: MAHARASHTRA
STATUS: VERIFIED
SOURCE
VERSION
EFFECTIVE DATE
```

The surrounding sentence should remain simple.

---

# 8. Content to Remove or Avoid

Do not introduce:

- zero hallucination claims
- 100% legal certainty
- tamper-proof claims
- certified immutable claims
- exaggerated coverage statistics
- fake performance metrics
- invented number of rules
- unsupported legal examples
- invented deadlines
- fabricated customer testimonials

A specific regulatory example should only be used if it comes from verified/demo-approved data.

---

# 9. Visual Storytelling Content Rule

Every chapter should have one sentence that the animation physically demonstrates.

Examples:

### “Start with what you do.”

Business sentence transforms into facts.

### “Only answer what changes the answer.”

Missing field becomes one question.

### “Find the rules that belong to you.”

Multiple sources narrow to relevant requirements.

### “See why it applies.”

Condition + source → result.

### “Turn requirements into next steps.”

Result → document/workflow/calendar.

This is mandatory for the premium product-film feeling.

---

# 10. Content Density

Do not increase paragraph length to explain the system.

If a concept can be shown through UI, remove the explanation.

Target:

- 1 headline
- 1 short supporting sentence
- 1 dominant product visual
- 1 interaction

per story chapter.

---

# 11. Final Content Standard

A normal visitor should understand the whole product story without knowing:

- RAG
- embeddings
- BM25
- AST
- reranking
- ontology
- knowledge graph

Those technologies may be shown in a technical architecture page or presentation.

The landing page should sell the outcome and demonstrate the mechanism.

Final emotional message:

> **You tell us about your business. We help you see what matters.**

Then:

> **Know what applies. Know what to do next.**
